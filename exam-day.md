# Test Day — Registration and Logistics

Do these early, not in the final week. Registering for the test is what turns "someday" into a
date; the whole plan assumes a real deadline.

## Register (this week)

- [ ] Find the official registration page for your test (exam board, certification body, school, or testing center).
- [ ] Check the name and ID requirements while registering — the name must match your ID exactly. Requirements change; don't rely on third-party summaries.
- [ ] Pick the delivery option (in person, online proctored, in class). In person = fewer things that can go wrong on the day; online = no travel, but strict room and connection checks.
- [ ] Book the date on or near your target. If it differs from the map's target, update `learner-map.md` → Deadline the same day.
- [ ] Read the cancellation/reschedule policy while booking.
- [ ] Check accommodations early if you want any; they need lead time and documentation.

## Scope re-check (final month)

- [ ] A month out: re-read the official syllabus / course outline / exam guide. If anything changed, update `learner-map.md` and tell your AI tutor the new scope.
- [ ] In the final week: check it again.

## Before the test

- Final 6 days: two full practice tests, Diagnostician on every miss.
- Day −2: light review only — your own cheat sheets, due flashcards. No new cards, no new topics.
- Test morning: 30 minutes of cheat sheets maximum, then stop. No studying in the last 2 hours before the test.

## On the day

- Verify the current ID requirements on the official provider's page; they change, and third-party lists go stale.
- Online proctored: closed-door room, clear desk, no headphones or second screens, stable connection. The proctor inspects the workspace. Log in ~30 minutes early for check-in.
- In person: arrive 15–30 minutes early.
- Bring nothing you can carry in your head: notes and phones stay out.

## At the keyboard

The strategy you drilled in Sparring:

- Pace: total minutes ÷ questions, from your Intake. That pace is exactly what sparring practised.
- Read each question twice. Hunt the constraint words: *best*, *most likely*, *least*, *without*, *only*.
- Eliminate 2 options first. Flag the unsure ones and move on, but always answer if there's no penalty for guessing.
- Your known failure mode is in the Intake. Don't change a right answer into a wrong one on a hunch.

## After

- [ ] Result and score into `daily-tracker.md` → After action (weakest/strongest area, root cause that mattered most).
- [ ] Pass: close the goal in the learner map. Not a pass: the mistake log and root causes ARE the retake plan — re-run the Mapmaker, check the retake policy, book the retake.
