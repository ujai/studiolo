# Code of conduct

Short version: be decent to people, and don't hand over anyone's private study data.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and plain.** Many people here are learners and teachers, not developers. Answer the
  question they asked, and explain any jargon you use.
- **Assume good faith.** Say what you disagree with and why. Aim at the work, not the person.
- **No harassment.** No insults, threats, slurs, unwanted sexual attention, or piling on. That
  goes for issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere this project gets discussed.
- **Keep private things private.** This project is about studying, so people will be tempted to
  paste their learner map, mistake log, mistakes, or exam accommodations into an issue. Don't
  ask for them, and don't post yours. Never post anyone else's.
- **Be careful with students.** Some learners using this are children. Don't ask a learner for
  personal details, and don't collect information about anyone.
- **Don't post other people's material.** Exam questions from paid, copyrighted, or
  NDA-protected question banks don't belong here in any form, not even reworded.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately. Open this repo's **Security** tab and click **Report a vulnerability**, or
use GitHub's [report abuse form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Please don't open a
public issue about it: the maintainer has no public email address, so GitHub is the way to reach
them.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts to keep this a place
people want to be. There's no ladder of warnings; that's the whole policy.
