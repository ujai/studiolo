# Cheat Sheets

One page per area. You fill them; the Clerk only tidies.

After intake, your agent copies the template below once per area into this folder
(`a1-<name>.md`, `a2-<name>.md`, …) with that area's topics as section hints.

## The rules

1. Fill each page from your head, not from the source. Deciding what to include IS the learning.
2. Don't copy other people's notes. Their structure is their memory, not yours.
3. After the weekly ritual, hand your messy notes to the Clerk (prompt §10): it reorders,
   groups, and shortens — it never adds. Anything it thinks is wrong gets `[CHECK]`.
4. You verify every `[CHECK]` in your authoritative source. No sheet carries an unverified fact.
5. In the days before a test, these pages are all you review. If it isn't on the page, either
   it doesn't matter or you don't know it yet.
